| — | **NOAA APT decoder rebuilt.** (1) Channels A/B came out swapped — the sync-offset calibration picked `min(P1,P2)` from a high-frequency-energy metric that cannot tell sync A (1040 Hz) from sync B (832 Hz); replaced by a matched filter for sync A (calibration and per-line). (2) Fixed vertical striping — the 2-pole 1 kHz IIR left ~5 % of the 2×subcarrier product, which the sample-and-hold decimation aliased; now a 41-tap FIR (fc 2 kHz) + linear-interpolation resampling. (3) Per-line contrast stretch replaced by an EMA 1st/99th-percentile. (4) Envelope-domain notches at 800/1600 Hz: at a real satellite's ±17 kHz deviation the radio's narrow-FM discriminator distorts and the subcarrier's 2nd/3rd harmonics alias through the 8 kHz audio path (3200/800 Hz, −26/−15 dB) — the notches remove most of the resulting striping. (5) Lock-loss re-sync (full-line search after two weak sync matches) so an audio gap no longer shifts the rest of the image.
